Privacy Policy

LumaLeaf stores plant records, light measurements, diagnosis answers, room spots, and checklist status locally on your device. Advertising is provided by Google Mobile Ads. In-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing.

Camera permission is requested only when you choose photo-based plant or diagnosis features. Notification permission is requested only when you turn on optional reminders. LumaLeaf does not sell personal information.
